  <h2>Research Overview</h2>
  <p>I am an archaeologist whose work bridges scientific and humanistic approaches to the study of past landscapes. Across my academic training and professional work, I have focused on addressing the longstanding divide between quantitative modeling and human-scale interpretation, treating them as complementary rather than competing approaches to archaeological knowledge.</p>

  <p>I hold a <a href="https://academicworks.cuny.edu/gc_etds/6509/">PhD in Anthropology</a> from the CUNY Graduate Center and a <a href="https://ucalgary.scholaris.ca/items/ad046493-9431-4fca-96c1-aebdc4496798">Master of Arts in Archaeology</a> from the University of Calgary. My academic training is grounded in an earlier undergraduate background in archaeology and philosophy, where I completed an honours thesis on shell midden sites in northeastern North America using legacy data to reconstruct site structure and formation processes.</p>

  <h2>Major Field Projects</h2>
  <p><strong>Yaxnohcah, Campeche (Yucatán Peninsula):</strong> My master’s research integrated GIS analysis with excavation data to investigate landscape hydrology and anthropogenic modification. This work examined how Maya communities reshaped the hydrological affordances of the region to expand their subsistence base and mitigate erosion, demonstrating that water management actively structured long-term landscape engineering.</p>

  <p><strong>Poverty Point, Louisiana:</strong> My doctoral research evaluated four core dimensions of this Late Archaic monumental site—layout, movement, visibility, and hydrology. By applying affordance theory, I demonstrated that abstract, landscape-scale models and experiential, human-scale observations generate distinct but mutually reinforcing insights into environmental structure and human experience.</p>

  <h2>Teaching & Academic Service</h2>
  <p>Alongside research, I maintain a strong commitment to teaching and academic service, with particular attention to supporting students with diverse learning needs and backgrounds. My approach emphasizes clarity, structure, and accessibility while maintaining analytical rigor across different environmental contexts, from tropical lowlands to riverine and coastal regions.</p>
